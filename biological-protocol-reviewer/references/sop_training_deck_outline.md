# SOP Training Deck Outline

Use only after `Review_Report.md` and `Revised_Protocol.md` are complete or
drafted enough to preserve readiness judgment. This is an outline/handoff, not a
PowerPoint-generation requirement.

## Slide spine

1. Protocol goal.
2. Critical path from sample/input to primary readout.
3. Required personnel, facility, instrument, and governance state.
4. Fragile steps and dominant failure modes.
5. QC gates and stop/go criteria.
6. Readout contracts and interpretation boundaries.
7. Operator burden: records that matter at the bench.
8. Mini-pilot/local validation plan.
9. Troubleshooting and rescue/exclude rules.
10. Final SOP checklist before execution.

## Rules

- Use the SOP's scientific argument and execution path, not the document section
  order, as the slide spine.
- Do not include decorative or publication-style figures unless they support a
  training decision.
- Do not omit safety/governance prerequisites from training material.
