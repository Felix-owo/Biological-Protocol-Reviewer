# Animal Experiment Review Module

## Core questions

- Is the biological question linked to a defined animal model and decisive readout?
- Is the experimental unit correctly identified: animal, litter, cage, dam, embryo,
  tissue, or repeated measure?
- Are strain, genetic background, genotype, sex, age, developmental stage, body
  weight, and health status specified?
- Are timed-pregnancy definitions explicit when embryonic stage matters?
- Are litter effects, cage effects, operator effects, and batch effects controlled?
- Are randomization, allocation concealment, blinding, inclusion/exclusion criteria,
  humane endpoints, and adverse-event monitoring described?
- Are route, formulation, vehicle, stability, storage, preparation time, dose record,
  and injection/administration record auditable?
- Are tissue harvest timing, dissection boundaries, sample IDs, and preservation
  conditions standardized?
- Are ethics approval, animal facility SOPs, analgesia/anesthesia requirements,
  and biosafety restrictions explicitly referenced?

## Required controls

- Vehicle-only control when vehicle can affect physiology.
- No-induction or no-treatment control for inducible systems.
- Genotype-negative control for reporter or transgene background.
- Positive control when assay sensitivity is being established.
- Sex-balanced or sex-justified design unless the biological question is sex-specific.
- Litter-aware design for embryo/neonatal studies.

## Common fatal flaws

- Treating embryos from the same dam as fully independent biological replicates
  without modeling litter/dam structure.
- Defining developmental time imprecisely.
- Ignoring sex as a biological variable without justification.
- No vehicle control for oil, solvent, hormone, antibiotic, inducer, anesthetic, or
  injury-related interventions.
- No objective criteria for excluding sick animals, resorbed embryos, failed
  injections, low-quality tissues, or failed genotyping.
- No monitoring plan for procedure-related morbidity.

## SOP upgrade requirements

Include animal record sheets for ID, genotype, sex, age, weight, group, cage,
litter/dam, intervention batch, operator, time, adverse events, endpoint, and tissue
IDs. Add a pregnancy or survival outcome table when relevant.
