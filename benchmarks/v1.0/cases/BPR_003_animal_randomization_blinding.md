# BPR_003_animal_randomization_blinding

A mouse treatment protocol compares treated and control groups using endpoint histology. The draft says that animals are assigned to groups "based on availability" and that the same operator performs dosing, sample processing, image acquisition, and scoring. It lists no allocation concealment, no blinding during scoring, no exclusion rule, no cage/litter balance, and no sample-size rationale.

Expected reviewer behavior: detect planning/reporting failures that can invalidate the endpoint readout and require ARRIVE/PREPARE-aligned randomization, blinding, experimental-unit definition, exclusion criteria, and sample-size justification before full execution.
